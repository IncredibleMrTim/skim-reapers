ABOUT SKIM REAPERS


THE NAME IS NEW. THE EXPERIENCE ISN'T.

Skim Reapers is the next chapter of an established plastering and dry-lining business built on 20 years of experience.
The new name represents our ambition to build something bigger — a professional company with the capability to take on increasingly substantial projects while maintaining the standards that have always mattered to us.

BUILT ON EXPERIENCE

I started plastering at 19, when my Dad — who now has more than 35 years' experience in the trade — taught me the basics.
At the time, I was working in high-end clothing retail. I enjoyed the work, but I wanted to learn a practical trade, develop new skills and build a career where I could work with my hands and see the results of my own efforts.
My Dad warned me:
"It's not for everyone. You will either love it or hate it!"
I loved it.
I spent the early days learning the fundamentals before gradually developing the speed, confidence and technical ability required to take on larger areas.
I eventually spent around 15 years working alongside my Dad, predominantly on commercial projects, while also taking on my own evening and weekend work.
During those years I developed experience in plastering, MF ceilings, studwork and dry-lining, as well as working on traditional and heritage projects where I learned about lime plastering and traditional construction techniques.

FROM SOLE TRADER TO SKIM REAPERS

I originally operated as a sole trader, building relationships with main contractors and gaining experience across a wide variety of projects.
In February 2020, I incorporated A Beaumont Plastering & Dry-lining Ltd.
The company subsequently began taking on larger projects directly, supported by an established network of skilled subcontractors.
Skim Reapers is the next stage of that journey.
The name has changed because the ambition has changed.
We're building a recognisable brand and a company capable of growing beyond a business centred around one individual.

GOOD ENOUGH ISN'T THE STANDARD.

We take genuine pride in the quality of our work.
Preparation matters. Attention to detail matters. Communication matters. Finishing a project properly matters.
We aim for work that is straight, flat, square, level and sharp, while maintaining a clean and organised working environment.
We don't believe quality should be sacrificed to achieve speed — but we also understand that deadlines matter.
When necessary, we'll work additional hours, weekends or increase labour to ensure projects are completed within the required timeframe.

BUILDING THE TEAM

As Skim Reapers grows, our focus is on building a team capable of delivering the same high standards consistently across every project.
That means selecting the right people, developing skills, sharing knowledge and maintaining strong quality control as the company expands.
The majority of our subcontractors are people we already know and trust. New members of the team are rigorously vetted, with previous work assessed before they are considered for Skim Reapers projects.

THE GOAL ISN'T SIMPLY TO BECOME BIGGER.

It's to build a company where the Skim Reapers standard is recognised regardless of who is holding the trowel.

BUILT FOR WHAT'S NEXT

The long-term ambition is to build Skim Reapers into a first-choice commercial plastering and dry-lining contractor for serious developers and major construction companies across the UK.
Growth isn't about becoming bigger for the sake of it.
It's about building a company capable of delivering exceptional work at scale — without losing the standards that built it.
