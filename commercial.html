PLANNED FROM THE START

Our involvement doesn't begin when the first board arrives on site.
We get involved early to understand what the project requires and identify potential issues before they become problems.

This includes:

  • Reviewing drawings and specifications
  • Assessing quantities
  • Site visits and surveys
  • Planning labour requirements
  • Coordinating materials and deliveries
  • Considering sequencing
  • Identifying potential clashes
  • Coordinating with other trades

WE DON'T CREATE PROBLEMS. WE CREATE SOLUTIONS.

Commercial construction rarely goes exactly to plan.

When challenges arise, our approach is to find practical solutions rather than create additional problems.

A PRACTICAL SOLUTION BEFORE THE PROBLEM BECAME A DELAY.

While installing an MF ceiling to an architect's specification, we identified that the proposed ceiling height would conflict with the positioning of the windows and building services.

Rather than simply flagging the issue, we developed an alternative window detail incorporating a sloped reveal to create a splay.
The proposal was presented to the main contractor, approved and implemented, allowing the works to continue while achieving a practical and considered finish.
