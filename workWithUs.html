SUBCONTRACTORS

We're looking for experienced plasterers and dry-liners who share our commitment to quality, reliability and professionalism.

We're particularly interested in people who:

  • Take genuine pride in their workmanship
  • Have a strong eye for detail
  • Are reliable and punctual
  • Communicate well
  • Work well as part of a team
  • Keep their work area clean and organised
  • Are willing to learn and develop
  • Can consistently meet the Skim Reapers standard

We don't necessarily expect everyone to have the same experience or skill level.
Attitude, reliability and a willingness to improve matter too.
